##### Introduction
Microsoft is expanding its offering with a long-awaited option : audio-only recording for meetings and calls. This new feature allows users to capture the audio of a meeting while preserving the anonymity of participants and the visual space, thus addressing confidentiality and compliance concerns.

---

##### Benefits
- Audio only : Recordings are available in MP4 format without video streaming (in the organizer's *OneDrive*), disabling cameras during capture.
- Privacy protected : Participants' faces and backgrounds are not recorded.
- Quality and compliance : The audio track remains the basis for transcripts, summaries, and other related AI features.

![image](/images/blog/teams/teams_meeting_record_audio_only_001.png)

---

##### Deployment
No configuration is required ; all Teams environments (Windows, Mac, web, iOS/Android) will automatically benefit from it since the end of November 2025.

---

##### Conclusion
The introduction of audio only recording in Microsoft Teams meetings is a significant step toward a more privacy-conscious and resource-efficient platform. Requiring no configuration, this option offers welcome flexibility and enhances Teams' appeal for sensitive or compliance-conscious use cases. Meeting recordings can now be efficient, lightweight, and confidential.
