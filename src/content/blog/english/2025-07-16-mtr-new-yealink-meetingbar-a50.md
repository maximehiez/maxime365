##### Introduction
In an increasingly hybrid work world, businesses are looking for video conferencing solutions that are powerful, easy to deploy, and seamlessly integrated into their *Microsoft 365* environment. With this in mind, *Yealink* offers the *MeetingBar A50*, an all-in-one video collaboration bar, certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms on Android, designed for medium to large meeting rooms.

---

##### A next-generation video experience
The MeetingBar A50 stands out with its 50MP triple camera system, featuring :
- A wide-angle lens to capture the entire room
- Two telephoto lenses to intelligently zoom in on participants

With *IntelliFocus*, the bar automatically zooms in on speakers, while *Video Fence* allows you to virtually frame relevant areas. It’s all optimized for *Teams Copilot* and multi-stream experiences with *IntelliFrame*, delivering optimal immersion in hybrid meetings.

![image](/images/blog/mtr/mtr_new_yealink_meetingbar_a50_001.png)

---

##### Professional audio quality
On the audio side, the MeetingBar A50 features :
- A 16-microphone array with a range of 10 meters
- AI-based noise and echo cancellation
- Full-duplex voice
- The ability to add up to three expansion microphones

It also features a four-speaker stereo bar, providing clear and powerful acoustic coverage, even in large rooms.

---

##### Easy deployment and centralized management
The MeetingBar A50 is designed for rapid deployment :
- A single PoE cable for power and data
- USB-C for *BYOD* (*Bring Your Own Device*) mode and 4K content sharing
- Compatibility with Android 13 and the *Microsoft Device Ecosystem Platform* (*MDEP*)

It is fully remotely manageable via Yealink or Microsoft cloud platforms, facilitating large-scale maintenance and monitoring.

![image](/images/blog/mtr/mtr_new_yealink_meetingbar_a50_002.png)

---

##### Security and Certification
The MeetingBar A50 is :
- Microsoft Teams Rooms Certified
- Compatible with Zoom Rooms and RingCentral
- Compliant with Microsoft security requirements via MDEP

It also offers an electronic privacy shutter for the camera, enhancing physical security in sensitive environments.

##### Conclusion
The Yealink MeetingBar A50 is a premium solution for businesses looking to equip their meeting rooms with a seamless, intelligent, and secure Teams experience. With its all-in-one design, advanced AI capabilities, and easy management, it perfectly meets the demands of modern hybrid environments.
