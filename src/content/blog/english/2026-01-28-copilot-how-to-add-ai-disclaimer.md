##### Introduction
Microsoft has enabled a setting in tenants that allows administrators to display the *Microsoft 365 Copilot* disclaimer in bold, and to attach a shortcut pointing to a usage policy (for example).

---

##### Prerequisites
**<u>Administrator role</u>**
- An account with the *Global Administrator* role to access the Microsoft 365 Admin Center.

---

##### Step 1 : Sign in to the Microsoft 365 Admin Center
Sign in to the Microsoft 365 Admin Center by opening your web browser to https://admin.cloud.microsoft.

---

##### Step 2 : Enable the disclaimer
In the left menu, click *<u>Copilot</u>*, then *<u>Settings</u>*, and *<u>Copilot actions</u>*.

![image](/images/blog/copilot/tuto/copilot_how_to_add_ai_disclaimer_001.png)

Click *<u>Copilot AI disclaimer</u>*, then *<u>Bold</u>* to enable the feature and enter the URL of the site to which you want to redirect the shortcut.

![image](/images/blog/copilot/tuto/copilot_how_to_add_ai_disclaimer_002.png)

---

##### Result
The result is visible in Teams. You can see the message ***AI-generated content may be incorrect***. Clicking the **i** at the end of the line opens a description, and clicking *Learn more* will redirect you to the specified URL.

![image](/images/blog/copilot/tuto/copilot_how_to_add_ai_disclaimer_003.png)

---

##### In which applications is the disclaimer available ?
The disclaimer is available in *Word*, *Excel*, *PowerPoint*, *Outlook*, *OneNote*, and *Microsoft 365 Copilot*. However, it is not available in *SharePoint*, *OneDrive*, *Whiteboard*, or *Forms*.

---

##### Conclusion
You now know how to activate a disclaimer in Copilot.
