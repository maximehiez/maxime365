##### Introduction
As of yesterday (October 14, 2025), *Exchange Server 2016* and *Exchange Server 2016* are no longer supported by Microsoft. Stability / performance bug fixes and security updates will no longer be deployed. Your servers will continue to work, but without official support, you are exposing yourself to vulnerabilities and technical issues.

---

##### What options are available to stay secure ?
Microsoft recommends two approaches :

**<u>Migrate to *Microsoft Exchange Online*</u>**

This is the simplest and most cost-effective solution. By switching to Exchange Online, you benefit from :
- A scalable and secure platform
- Advanced features like Copilot for productivity
- Native integration with Microsoft 365

<br/>

**<u>Upgrade to *Exchange Server Subscription Edition* (SE)</u>**

Available since July 2025, this edition allows businesses that wish to remain on-premises to continue using Exchange with a subscription model.
- If you are currently using Exchange 2019, you can upgrade in-place to SE.
- If you are currently using Exchange 2015, it is recommended that you first migrate to 2019, then to SE.

---

##### Conclusion
The end of support for Exchange Server marks an important step in the transition to modern solutions. Whether you choose Exchange Online or Exchange SE, the goal is clear : to ensure the security, performance, and continuity of your communications. The future is cloud-based and AI-powered — and it's time to make the leap.
