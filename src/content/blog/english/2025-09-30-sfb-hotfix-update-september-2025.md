##### Introduction
Microsoft continues to actively support *Skype for Business Server*, particularly its *Subscription Edition* (SE), with regular patch releases. The September 2025 hotfix provides fixes to address two issues impacting functionality.

---

##### Fixes made
The September 2025 hotfix fixes the following issues :
- MACP icons : *Modern Admin Control Panel* (*MACP*) interface icons were not loading when the server was accessed without an internet connection - KB5067476
- Export-CsRGSConfiguration command : The PowerShell command *Export-CsRGSConfiguration* was not executing properly on Skype for Business SE Server Edition - KB5067477

---

##### Conclusion
The September 2025 hotfix fixes two minor issues with your Skype for Business Server SE infrastructure, but it's still worth applying.
