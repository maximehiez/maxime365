##### Introduction
*Teams Premium* lets organizations replace the standard meeting reactions with custom visuals in their brand colors. Administrators upload the visual assets and define themes, while users can select those themes when scheduling their meetings.

---

##### Prerequisites
**<u>Required licenses</u>**
- *Microsoft Teams Premium*.

**<u>Administrator role</u>**
- An account with the *Global Administrator* or *Teams Administrator* role to access the Microsoft Teams Admin Center.

---

##### Step 1 : Sign in to the Microsoft Teams Admin Center
Sign in to the Microsoft Teams Admin Center by opening your web browser to https://admin.teams.microsoft.com.

---

##### Step 2 : Create a new theme
In the left menu, click *<u>Meetings</u>*, then *<u>Customization policies</u>*, and edit the *Global (Org-wide default)* policy or create a new one.

Replace one or more reactions with your new images.

![image](/images/blog/teams/tuto/teams_how_to_upload_branded_meeting_reactions_001.png)

The new theme appears in your meeting visuals and is applied automatically.

![image](/images/blog/teams/tuto/teams_how_to_upload_branded_meeting_reactions_002.png)

<Notice type="note">The image must be a PNG with a transparent background, 1024px by 1024px.</Notice>

---

##### Validate the result
The new theme is applied within a few minutes. Here, the single replaced reaction appears in the banner.

![image](/images/blog/teams/tuto/teams_how_to_upload_branded_meeting_reactions_003.png)

Remote participants receive it as well.

![image](/images/blog/teams/tuto/teams_how_to_upload_branded_meeting_reactions_004.png)

---

##### Conclusion
You now know how to upload custom reactions in Teams.
