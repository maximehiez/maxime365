##### Introduction
The *Logitech Rally Board 65* is an all-in-one video conferencing solution designed to simplify meetings and collaboration in business environments. With its 65-inch touchscreen, it offers an immersive and easy-to-deploy video conferencing experience. Here's a detailed look at its features and benefits for Microsoft Teams Rooms (MTR).

![image](/images/blog/mtr/mtr_logitech_rally_board_65_001.png)

---

##### Key Features of the Logitech Rally Board 65
**<u>65-inch touchscreen</u>**

The Rally Board 65 features a 65-inch touchscreen that allows for intuitive and seamless interaction. This display offers 4K Ultra HD resolution, ensuring exceptional image quality for video meetings.
<br/>

**<u>High-quality audio and video</u>**

The Rally Board 65 incorporates advanced audio and video technologies, including :
- Microphone array : A set of front-facing microphones for clear audio capture.
- Digital PTZ camera : A camera with digital zoom for full room coverage.
- Full-range speakers : Powerful speakers for optimal sound quality.
- AI technologies : *RightSight 2* for automatic participant framing and *RightSound 2* for background noise suppression.
<br/>

**<u>Easy and flexible installation</u>**

The Rally Board 65 is designed for quick and easy setup. It can be configured with the camera positioned above or below the screen, ensuring natural eye contact between participants. The included brackets and remote setup allow the Rally Board 65 to be set up in minutes, minimizing the need for on-site technical support.

![image](/images/blog/mtr/mtr_logitech_rally_board_65_002.png)

---

##### Benefits for Microsoft Teams Rooms
**<u>Microsoft Teams integration</u>**

The Rally Board 65 is fully compatible with Microsoft Teams, providing seamless integration for meetings and collaboration. Users can start Teams meetings with a simple tap, easily share content, and control the room from the touchscreen.
<br/>

**<u>Configuration flexibility</u>**

The Rally Board 65 can be deployed in a variety of environments, from small huddle rooms to large open spaces. It is designed to adapt to the specific needs of each space, offering maximum flexibility for room configurations.
<br/>

**<u>Advanced AI technologies</u>**

Built-in AI technologies, such as RightSight 2 and RightSound 2, ensure optimal participant alignment and clear sound quality, even in noisy environments. The Depth Blur feature uses a depth mapping sensor to distinguish sharp areas from blurred areas, providing a natural depth effect.
