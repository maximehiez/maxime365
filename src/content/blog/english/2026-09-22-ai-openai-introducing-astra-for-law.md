##### Introduction
*OpenAI* announces *Astra for Law*, a foundation that lets law firms and legal technology companies build AI products and workflows around their expertise. The offering combines *GPT-6 Astra* with parameters, tools, and context tailored to professional legal work.

Check the GPT-6 Astra article [HERE](https://maxime.hiez.ca/en/blog/2026-09-08-ai-openai-introducing-gpt-6-astra).

---

##### A dedicated legal research index
Astra for Law relies on a new legal research index that complements the licensed content and specialized products firms already use. The index covers US case law, statutes, regulations, court procedural rules, and administrative decisions across a corpus of more than 230 million URLs, refreshed daily. A partnership with *Free Law Project*, the organization behind *CourtListener*, adds to this a case law collection covering more than 99.9% of published precedential US case law.

On the 200 legal research questions from the *Legal Research Bench* by *Vals AI*, at maximum reasoning effort :
- <u>Overall accuracy</u> : 54.0% for Astra for Law, compared with 38.7% for GPT-6 Astra using web search alone, a 40% relative improvement.
- <u>Landmark cases found</u> : +24% compared with GPT-6 Astra using web search alone.
- <u>Relevant passages identified</u> : Up to 54% more relevant passages found in the right court decisions.

---

##### Beyond research
Custom legal analysis and drafting instructions guide the model in applying this research to the facts of a matter : building arguments or contract clauses, identifying weaknesses and uncertainties, distinguishing the rule of law a court relied on from its other observations.

Several firms have already built tools on this foundation with OpenAI's engineers :
- <u>Sullivan & Cromwell</u> : A contract analyzer that cross-references a new transaction against the firm's negotiation guides and precedents.
- <u>Ropes & Gray</u> : A deal due-diligence system that links data room findings back to their source.
- <u>Cooley</u> : *GO Public*, an IPO preparation tool that automatically carries changes to a deal through the entire filing.
- <u>Harvey</u> : Reports that Astra for Law *"performed well on core aspects of legal research : grounding answers in directly relevant sources, citation accuracy, and practical advisory guidance"*.

---

##### Trust and governance
Law firms have to protect their clients' confidential information. OpenAI created a dedicated *Trusted Access* program, which for eligible firms includes a zero data retention policy on the API and exclusion by default from human review on *ChatGPT Enterprise*. A partnership with *Latham & Watkins* shapes the design of access permissions, ethical walls, and firm oversight.

The launch also comes with 26 partner plugins connecting ChatGPT to the tools firms already use, including *iManage*, *Intapp*, *DeepJudge*, and *Thomson Reuters*, along with 9 community plugins and 47 customizable skills.

---

##### Availability
Astra for Law is first being offered to a limited number of law firms through Trusted Access in *ChatGPT* and *Codex*, with API access planned soon. The model appears as *GPT-6 Astra Law* in the model picker, and under the identifier *gpt-6-astra-law* in the API.

---

##### Conclusion
Astra for Law follows the same logic as Foundry or Copilot for integrating GPT-6 Astra : rather than a generic model, a specialized foundation connected to the sources that carry authority in the targeted domain. The 40% relative gain on legal research accuracy, combined with the early access already granted to firms like Sullivan & Cromwell and Cooley, positions Astra for Law as a direct competitor to existing specialized legal offerings rather than a simple ChatGPT skin.
