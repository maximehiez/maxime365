##### Introduction
The *Gartner Magic Quadrant* for *Unified Communications as a Service* (*UCaaS*) is an essential tool for organizations looking to evaluate cloud communications service providers. This report highlights market leaders, challengers, visionaries and niche players, providing an overview of different vendors' capabilities and strategies.

---

##### Seven consecutive years in the lead
Microsoft has been named a Leader in the Gartner 2025 Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS), marking its seventh consecutive year in this position. This recognition confirms Microsoft's ability to effectively execute and deliver a comprehensive vision for the future of unified communications.

![image](/images/blog/teams/teams_gartner_ucaas_2025_001.png)

---

##### Why this recognition ?
This distinction reflects the continued innovations made to Microsoft Teams, which is becoming the AI-powered work platform : simple, intelligent, and secure.

Notable new features include :
- Agents in meetings : Virtual assistants like *Facilitator* or *Interpreter* for more productive and inclusive meetings.
- New chat and channels experience : Redesigned interface to simplify navigation and organize conversations.
- Copilot integrated into calls : Automatic generation of summaries for call transfers, improving continuity of exchanges.

---

##### Simplifying communications
Teams continues to reduce complexity with features such as:
- UCaaS + CCaaS telephony integration via Dynamics 365 and ISV partners
- SMS support (US and Canada) directly in Teams
- Advanced supervisor tools in the Queues app (listening, coaching, call handling)

---

##### Smarter and more secure collaboration
- Copilot and personalized agents to automate tasks and anticipate needs.
- Enhanced security : OTP verification for guests, detection of sensitive content during screen sharing, and automatic updating of sensitivity labels.

---

##### AI at the heart of the workplace
With *Microsoft 365 Copilot* and innovations like voice recognition in Teams Rooms, intelligent space and device management, Microsoft is redefining modern collaboration.

---

##### Conclusion
This recognition from Gartner confirms that Microsoft isn't just following trends, it's creating them. Combining AI, security, and simplicity, Teams is establishing itself as the go-to platform for organizations looking to modernize their communications and improve productivity. The future of unified communications will be intelligent, collaborative, and secure, and Microsoft is clearly leading this transformation.
