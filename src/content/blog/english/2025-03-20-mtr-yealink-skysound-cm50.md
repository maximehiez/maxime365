##### Introduction
*Enterprise Connect* is an annual conference that brings together communications technology professionals, innovators, and others. This event showcases technological advances in areas such as unified communications, contact centers, AI-powered solutions, etc.<br/>
This year's event (March 17-20, 2025) just wrapped up at the Gaylord Palms Resort & Convention Center in Orlando, Florida, and many innovations were showcased.

I'll start with the *Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante* kit, certified for Microsoft Teams.

---

##### The Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante kit
The Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante audio kit includes a powerful CM50 ceiling microphone, a CS10-D ceiling speaker, and an AP08 digital signal processor (DSP). It provides premium audio coverage thanks to its advanced Dante beamforming technology and AI-based sound optimization capabilities, which deliver an immersive sound experience. The Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante kit guarantees precise and clear audio capture, ideal for large meeting rooms, boardrooms, and divisible spaces.

![image](/images/blog/mtr/mtr_yealink_skysound_cm50_001.png)

---

##### Yealink CM50 key features
**<u>Dante beamforming technology</u>**

The Yealink CM50 uses Dante beamforming technology, which enables accurate, high-fidelity audio capture with low latency. This technology is ideal for large meeting rooms.
<br/><br/>

**<u>Wide audio coverage</u>**

Equipped with 127 wide-field microphones, the Yealink CM50 provides superior audio coverage within a 5-meter radius. It allows you to create customized pickup zones, reducing unwanted background noise.
<br/><br/>

**<u>AI-based sound optimization</u>**

The Yealink CM50 incorporates an AI-based sound optimization model that combines intelligent noise reduction and reverberation cancellation. This ensures every word is heard clearly and distinctly. <br/><br/>

**<u>Integration with multi-camera solutions</u>**

The Yealink CM50 can precisely locate the sound source, enabling accurate speaker tracking during meetings. It integrates seamlessly with Yealink's smart camera solutions, providing a real-time speaker tracking experience.

![image](/images/blog/mtr/mtr_yealink_skysound_cm50_002.png)

---

##### Benefits of the Yealink CM50
- Premium audio quality : Thanks to its wide-field microphones and beamforming technology, the Yealink CM50 delivers exceptional audio quality.
- Customization and flexibility : Custom capture zones and integration with multi-camera solutions allow for maximum flexibility in meeting room configurations.
- Easy integration : Compatible with Yealink MTR systems and other Dante-enabled devices, the Yealink CM50 integrates easily into existing environments.

##### Recommended usage
The Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante kit is recommended for medium to large meeting rooms, boardrooms, training rooms, and divisible spaces. It is designed to deliver an immersive and seamless audio experience in a variety of meeting scenarios.

##### Conclusion
The Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante kit is a cutting-edge audio solution for business environments, offering superior audio quality and smart integration with existing meeting systems. If you need a reliable and high-performance audio solution for your meetings, the Yealink SkySound CM50 Dante kit is an excellent choice.
