##### Introduction
A few weeks ago, I showed you how to enable DLP to prevent the copying of financial data to an external cloud storage solution using *Microsoft Purview*, thus preventing accidental or malicious data leaks (*Data Loss Prevention*). Continuing with the idea of talking to you about DLP, here this time is how to protect the printing of financial data.

Check the article from January 2026 [HERE](https://maxime.hiez.ca/en/blog/2026-01-06-purview-how-to-enable-dlp-cloud-storage-block-financial-data).

---

##### Prerequisites
**<u>Required licenses</u>**
- *Microsoft 365 E5*.
- *Microsoft Purview Suite* in addition to another license (*E3*, *Business*, ...).

**<u>Administrator role</u>**
- An account with the *Global Administrator* or *Compliance Administrator* role to access the Microsoft Purview Portal.

---

##### Step 1 : Sign in to the Microsoft Purview Portal
Sign in to the Microsoft Purview Portal by opening your web browser to https://purview.microsoft.com.

---

##### Step 2 : Create a DLP policy for devices
In the left menu, click *<u>Solutions</u>*, then *<u>Data Loss Prevention</u>*.

Click *<u>Create policy</u>*.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_001.png)

Set a name and description.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_002.png)

Apply this policy for devices.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_003.png)

Configure the various data type attributes and notifications. In my example, I chose an existing type (Canadian financial data).

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_004.png)

Enable the policy to put it into production.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_005.png)

<Notice type="note">The policy can take up to 24 hours to take effect.</Notice>

---

##### Step 3 : Print financial data to validate the policy
I'm reusing the Word file *<u>Cards.docx</u>* containing a (<u>fake</u>) credit card, which I created for my previous article.

I don't have a printer, but I'm printing it to PDF and ...

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_006.png)

... the attempt is blocked. The popup configured in the policy appears warning that this is data that cannot be printed.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_007.png)

---

##### Step 4 : Receive the notification
If you have configured notifications, an email is sent to the designated people within the next few seconds.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_008.png)

---

##### What if I want to allow / block certain printers ?
In the left menu, click *<u>Settings</u>*, then *<u>Data Loss Prevention</u>*.

Click *<u>Printer groups</u>* and add the target printer(s).

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_009.png)

Edit the DLP policy by adding the printer group and the action to apply.

![image](/images/blog/purview/tuto/purview_how_to_enable_dlp_print_block_financial_data_010.png)

---

##### Conclusion
With Microsoft Purview DLP, organizations can automatically detect, monitor, and block unauthorized printing of critical data, such as credit card numbers or bank account information.<br/><br/>
You now know how to enable DLP for printing.
