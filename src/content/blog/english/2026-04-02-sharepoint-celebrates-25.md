##### Introduction
Microsoft has announced the 25th anniversary of *Microsoft SharePoint*, a platform that now plays a central role in *Microsoft 365*. Since its initial release in 2001, SharePoint has evolved significantly ; from an on-premises document portal to a cloud platform integrated with all Microsoft services.

This anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on its evolution and its current role.

---

##### Origin and evolution
Originally, SharePoint was designed as a solution for :
- Centralized document management
- Intranet
- Internal collaboration

![image](/images/blog/sharepoint/sharepoint_celebrates_25_001.png)

The initial versions were deployed exclusively on-premises, with a strong reliance on local infrastructure.

Key development milestones :
- SharePoint Portal Server (early 2000s)
- Integration with Microsoft Office
- Launch of *SharePoint Online* with Microsoft 365
- Modernization of sites and user experience
<br/><br/>

Today, SharePoint is primarily used via its cloud version.

---

##### Position in Microsoft 365
SharePoint has become a fundamental component of Microsoft 365. It is used as :
- Storage backend for Microsoft Teams
- Document repository for Microsoft OneDrive
- Publishing platform for intranets

Specifically :
- Every Teams team is based on a SharePoint site
- Every OneDrive user uses SharePoint in the background

This makes it a cross-functional component, often invisible to users.

---

##### Integration with the Microsoft ecosystem
SharePoint is now deeply integrated with :
- Microsoft Teams (storage and collaboration)
- Microsoft OneDrive (personal storage)
- Microsoft Power Automate (automation)
- Microsoft Power Apps (business applications)

This integration allows you to build complete scenarios without leaving the Microsoft environment.

---

##### Current challenges
Despite its evolution, SharePoint still faces several challenges :
- Data governance
- Permission management
- Content organization
- User adoption

In many environments, SharePoint is used extensively, but sometimes without a clear structure.

---

##### AI and agent experiences
Microsoft continues to simplify the creation of comprehensive solutions for critical business needs, from procurement contracts to marketing content, using natural language. SharePoint now acts as an active partner, asking clarifying questions and proposing plans to ensure the solution meets expectations even before it's built. By describing the requirements, SharePoint can offer a structured plan covering site structure, pages, libraries, lists, and starter content.

Coming soon, custom AI skills, based on knowledge of your organization (standards, terminology, governance rules, and business logic), will enable AI to provide relevant and tailored assistance for your operations.

![image](/images/blog/sharepoint/sharepoint_celebrates_25_002.png)

---

##### Conclusion
After 25 years, SharePoint remains a key component of the Microsoft ecosystem. Its evolution to the cloud and its integration with Microsoft 365 have made it a central platform, often used without being visible. Rather than a simple document management tool, SharePoint is now an essential building block of modern collaborative architectures.
