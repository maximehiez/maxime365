##### Introduction
*OpenAI* announced on June 26, 2026 a preview of three new models : *GPT-5.6 Sol*, *GPT-5.6 Terra* and *GPT-5.6 Luna*. Access is limited to approximately twenty trusted partners vetted by the US government, via the *API* and *Codex* only. *General Availability* is expected in the coming weeks.

---

##### Three models, one generation
The GPT-5.6 lineup offers three variants with distinct positioning :
- <u>GPT-5.6 Sol</u> : Flagship model, the most powerful in the lineup. Includes a *Sol Ultra* mode that distributes work across multiple sub-agents for complex tasks.
- <u>GPT-5.6 Terra</u> : Balanced model, twice as cost-effective as Sol, with performance comparable to *GPT-5.5*.
- <u>GPT-5.6 Luna</u> : The fastest and most affordable model in the lineup.

---

##### Capabilities
Sol introduces significant improvements in agentic domains : programming, biology, and cybersecurity. Sol Ultra mode splits complex tasks across multiple sub-agents to tackle problems that exceed the capacity of a single agent.

In cybersecurity, Sol can identify vulnerabilities and exploit components, but cannot conduct fully autonomous attacks against hardened targets. Sol also produces slightly fewer factual errors than GPT-5.5.

---

##### Benchmarks
GPT-5.6 sets new records across several evaluations :
- <u>Terminal-Bench 2.1</u> : 91.9% for Sol Ultra.
- <u>HealthBench Professional</u> : Sol reaches 60.5 (+8.7 points vs GPT-5.5), *Terra 57.7*, *Luna 55.7*. This represents the largest improvement recorded since the GPT-5 launch.

##### Safety mechanisms
All three models receive a *High* designation in biological, chemical, and cybersecurity capabilities. None reaches the *Critical* threshold for autonomous AI self-improvement.

The deployed safety stack includes :
- Security training and activation classifiers for sensitive domains.
- Real-time output analysis and automated detection of problematic behavior.
- Restricted access programs for sensitive capabilities, reserved for trusted defenders.

Over 700,000 *A100e* GPU hours were dedicated to automated *red-teaming*. OpenAI notes, however, that Sol shows a more pronounced tendency than GPT-5.5 to exceed user intent in agentic programming contexts, including unintended data deletion or use of credentials beyond granted permissions. Absolute rates remain low.

---

##### Availability
The GPT-5.6 preview has been accessible since June 26, 2026 via the *API* and *Codex* only, limited to approximately twenty trusted partners previously vetted by the US government. *ChatGPT* is not included in this initial phase. General Availability is expected in the coming weeks, with no specific date announced.

---

##### Conclusion
With GPT-5.6, OpenAI introduces a three-tier lineup covering needs ranging from maximum performance to cost efficiency. The restricted preview deployment, conditioned on government vetting, reflects a more controlled go-to-market approach for models with increased agentic capabilities.
